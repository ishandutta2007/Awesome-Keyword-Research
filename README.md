# Awesome-Keyword-Research

Top Keyword Research Tools Ecosystem

Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects
Focused on SEO Keyword Discovery, SERP Analysis, Volume Estimation & Content Planning
Last updated: September 2026

This repository tracks notable SaaS platforms and open-source projects for Keyword Research. These tools help SEO professionals, content marketers, and indie builders discover search terms, estimate search volume, analyze keyword difficulty, cluster topics by intent, and uncover content gaps.

Examples include Ahrefs, Semrush, Moz Keyword Explorer, KWFinder, KeywordTool.io, SpyFu, Long Tail Pro, SECockpit, LowFruits, and Serpstat (the category leaders).

Open-source emphasis: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosting, custom data source integration, and transparent SEO workflows — ideal for indie builders, small agencies, and developers who want full control over their keyword data without expensive subscriptions.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

SaaS/Hosted Platforms

Ahrefs
Comprehensive SEO toolkit with one of the largest backlink and keyword databases. Features Keyword Explorer, Site Explorer, and Rank Tracker.

Semrush
All-in-one search marketing platform with keyword research, competitor analysis, PPC intelligence, and content optimization tools.

Moz Keyword Explorer
Keyword research tool from Moz with volume, difficulty, and organic CTR metrics. Integrated with Moz Pro's broader SEO suite.

KWFinder
User-friendly keyword research tool by Mangools, focused on finding low-competition long-tail keywords.

KeywordTool.io
Keyword suggestion tool that generates hundreds of long-tail keyword ideas from Google, YouTube, Bing, Amazon, and more.

SpyFu
Competitor research and keyword intelligence platform, particularly strong for PPC keyword analysis and ad history.

Long Tail Pro
Keyword research software specializing in long-tail keyword discovery and competitor analysis.

SECockpit
Cloud-based keyword research tool with multi-source scraping (Google, YouTube, Amazon), built-in rank tracker, and traffic/conversion estimation. Processes up to 200 keywords per minute.-
3

LowFruits
Keyword research tool focused on finding low-competition, easy-to-rank keywords with a simple workflow.

Serpstat
All-in-one search marketing platform from Ukraine with keyword research, competitor analysis, site audit, and rank tracking modules.-
2

Mangools
Suite of SEO tools including KWFinder, SERPChecker, and LinkMiner for affordable keyword research.

Ubersuggest
Free/freemium keyword research tool by Neil Patel with volume, difficulty, and content ideas.

Open-Source GitHub Projects

All-In-One Free SEO Tool
Free, self-hosted SEO platform positioning itself as an open-source alternative to Ahrefs, Semrush, and Moz. Includes 99 tools: site audits, rank tracking, keyword research, AI search visibility, content briefs, and backlinks. No paid APIs required — uses headless Chromium for SERP scraping. License: MIT.-
9

ZensInk SEO Toolkit
Zero-dependency Python CLI toolkit for indie builders. Twenty tools covering keyword research, clustering, difficulty scoring (via SERP structure), search volume (Bing Webmaster API), competitor gap analysis, and site audits. Explicitly designed as a free alternative to Ahrefs/Semrush. License: MIT.-
12

HasData Python-for-SEO
Professional SEO research toolkit with 7 specialized Python tools: Google Suggest Harvester, Trends Breakout Analyzer, PAA Tree Builder, SERP Intent Classifier, SERP Similarity Matrix, Content Gap Analyzer, and AI Overview Monitor. Powered by HasData API. License: Open source.-

SEO Machine (TheCraigHewitt)
Open-source content workflow tool that orchestrates multi-agent pipelines for keyword research, competitor analysis, and SEO-friendly article generation. Integrates with Google Analytics 4, Google Search Console, and DataForSEO. License: Open source.-

Additional Strong Open-Source Options

Keyword Extraction Libraries: Community libraries for RAKE, TextRank, YAKE, and other keyword extraction algorithms from text content.

SerpBear: Self-hosted rank tracking and keyword monitoring with unlimited tracking (MIT).

SEO Panel: Older but functional open-source SEO management platform with keyword tracking.

Google Autocomplete Scrapers: Numerous lightweight scripts for pulling long-tail suggestions from Google, YouTube, Amazon, and Bing.

Frameworks for building custom systems: Combine Python (requests + BeautifulSoup), SQLite for keyword storage, Streamlit or Next.js for dashboards, and Ollama for local AI-powered intent classification.
